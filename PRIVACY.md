# Privacy Policy for Data Dictionary Validator

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

**Data Dictionary Validator** operates entirely locally within your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or webpage content on external servers.

## Data Processed Locally
* **Uploaded Data Dictionaries:** Any `.xlsx` spreadsheet files you upload are parsed locally in your browser using SheetJS and stored exclusively in your local browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`) for your convenience across sessions.
* **Selected Webpage Text:** When you explicitly click "Validate Selected Text" or press `Alt+Shift+V`, the extension reads only the text you currently have highlighted on the active tab to check it against your local dictionary. This text is never logged or sent outside your browser.
* **User Preferences:** Your selected validation language (English or French) is saved locally in your browser.

## Third-Party Services & Analytics
This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or external APIs.
